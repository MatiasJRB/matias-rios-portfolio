## 📈 Cómo Recopilar las Métricas

```
Mensaje sugerido:
"Hi [Manager Name],
I'm updating my portfolio and would love to include some impact metrics from my time at [Company]. Could you help me with any specific numbers around:
- Performance improvements I contributed to
- Business metrics that improved during my tenure
- Team efficiency gains
Any approximate numbers would be greatly appreciated for my professional documentation."
```

- Lines of code contributed
- Pull requests merged
- Issues resolved

- Solicitar recommendations específicas con números

- Project completion reports

- Incident reduction reports
